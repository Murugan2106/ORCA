PRODUCT INFORMATION • NEUROMODULATION

LivaNova VNS Therapy™

A different approach to supporting epilepsy care.

VNS Therapy is an adjunctive treatment option for people with drug-resistant epilepsy. It uses an implantable pulse generator to deliver programmed electrical stimulation to the vagus nerve as part of an ongoing treatment plan. If you are looking for information about VNS Therapy, our team can help connect you with the right product and support information.

STALWART Reusable Laparoscopic Instruments

Instruments for the everyday needs of minimally invasive surgery.
